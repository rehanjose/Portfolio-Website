01 / Event Context

What is Converge?

NIFT Converge is the annual national inter-campus festival uniting thousands of student designers, artists, performers, and athletes across 19 NIFT campuses throughout India.

Hosted by NIFT Kannur in the coastal heart of Kerala—the historic city of Looms & Lores—Converge 2025 served as a 3-day confluence celebrating four decades of design excellence, artistic expression, and fierce athletic competition. The official playbook had to act as both a celebratory keepsake and a clear, fast-scanning operational rulebook for every visiting contingent.

02 / Visual Identity

The Design Theme: Rooted in Kannur.

Every visual choice inside the publication draws directly from the spirit of Kerala and Kannur’s coastal heritage—bridging folk tradition, historic architecture, and contemporary athletic dynamism.

Inspiration Board

Regional Ethos

Drawing visual cues from Kerala’s cultural tapestry—Thrissur Pooram elephants, Kalaripayattu martial traditions, the Great Indian Hornbill, St. Angelo Fort, and the verdant amphitheatre of NIFT Kannur.

Symbol Anatomy

The Converge Mark

The event emblem synthesizes the fluid trunk of the Kerala elephant (direction & momentum), concentric amphitheatre rings (the gathering stage), 19 campus dots, and an ascending bird of creative flight.

7-Color Chromatic System · Used for Section Wayfinding & Event Category Coding

Primary Rounded Display
Comfortaa

Youthful, modern, and energetic rounded letterforms for chapter titles, section dividers, and cover typography.

Secondary Editorial & Body
Jakarta Sans

Crisp grotesque sans-serif engineered for tabular clarity, quick scanning, and dense competition rulebook copy.

05 / Colophon & Production

Print Specifications & Team Credits.

Engineered for tactile durability across three days of campus-wide use—combining a velvety soft-touch cover with spot UV highlights and section-sewn lay-flat binding so wide schedule tables stay completely legible across the gutter.

Dimensions 203 × 203 mm (8" × 8" Square)
Extent 96 Interior Pages + 4 Covers
Cover Finishes Velvet Soft-Touch + Spot UV Gloss
Binding Section-Sewn PUR Lay-Flat 180°
  1. 01. Modular Grid Discipline at Scale — Designing a 96-page multi-category rulebook required reusable master templates so cultural essays, sports regulations, and dense timetables all felt part of one cohesive editorial voice.
  2. 02. Information Hierarchy Under Pressure — Participants needed to check eligibility, scoring rubrics, and venues in seconds; color-coded headers, pictograms, and tabular typography turned dense rules into scannable spreads.
  3. 03. Cross-Disciplinary Team Collaboration — Integrating logo architecture, 36 custom icons, and 3D apparel techpacks from fellow designers taught me how editorial layout acts as the unifying glue of a brand system.
