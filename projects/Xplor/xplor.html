About Xplor

Xplor AI Mobility is an intelligent transit technology company building connected digital infrastructure to transform public mobility. Its platform integrates transportation operations, fleet management, ticketing, scheduling, and live tracking—bringing together private bus operators, public transit networks, and metro feeder services into a unified, data-driven mobility ecosystem.
