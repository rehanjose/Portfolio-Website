MAZHAYATHONAM

Mazhayathonam, meaning Onam in the midst of heavy rain, captures the essence of celebrating despite adverse weather. In 2022, during the Onam festival, Kochi experienced severe flooding, leaving many people saddened. This digital sketch concept portrays a girl dressed in a traditional pattu saree, visibly angry and frustrated because the heavy rains led to the cancellation of her college’s Onam celebrations. The image reflects the disappointment felt by many due to the disruption of this cherished festival.
